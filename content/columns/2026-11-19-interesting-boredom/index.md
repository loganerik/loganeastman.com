It has become increasingly difficult to do nothing with conviction. Even a moment of waiting arrives already filled with the expectation that we will be looking at something.

## A room for unoccupied thought

I wonder whether boredom, handled gently, is less a state to escape than a place to visit.

{{< ornament >}}

### A small experiment

The next time a queue seems intolerably long, try doing only the waiting. The results are inconclusive, which is why I recommend it.
