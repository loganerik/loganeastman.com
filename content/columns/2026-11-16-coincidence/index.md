I have never been especially good at leaving things unrelated. Give me two stray details and I will find a small bridge between them, whether or not they were ever meant to meet.

## The pleasure of an accidental pattern

Perhaps the thing that makes an ordinary coincidence worth saving is the modest permission it gives us to imagine that the world is in conversation with itself.

### Two unrelated things

An old receipt, a line from a book, and the song in the next room are perfectly capable of sitting side by side without meaning anything at all. I have nevertheless found myself listening for a connection.

{{< pullquote >}}A coincidence is sometimes just an invitation to pay attention.{{< /pullquote >}}
