## Reading a newspaper

They don't know I have cervical myofascial pain due to tech neck and/or health OCD. Or that I'm recommitting myself to intentional content consumption. That holding up the *FT Weekend* to my face lessens looking down toward my phone and passively reading clickbait, thus reducing the pain and regulating my nervous system (in theory). 

So, I look like one of those "performative men" waving around a newspaper on a spindle for all of Comet over Delphi to see. As if their trays of cortado, biscotti, sugar cube, stir stick and soda water are any less obnoxious (that's also what I ordered). 

Even worse, grabbing the paper off the wall by the doorway made it seem like an insincere impulse to the long line of patrons. In fact, I had first walked to North Figueroa Bookshop looking for any sort of daily. I had searched for a nearby newsstand, using some of my few precious allotted moments of phone viewing, but the only ones in the city seem to be outside Vroman's in Pasadena or Kosher News on Fairfax. I probably would've had better luck looking inside of El Super grocery store, but I didn't think of that until later. 

At any rate, there may be a promising treatment for pancreatic cancer. 

What's the world coming to when you can't find a newspaper to read? Or that, socially, you're damned if you do and damned if you don't?
