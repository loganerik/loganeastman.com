## There's no place like myofascial release

Western medicine is frustrating and insurance is awful, but is all the tension in my body really from all those years of not letting myself truly be heard? Will putting each hand on opposite knee and bending downward really release the quadratus lumborum and put an end to my chronic shoulder pain? That's all I had to do this whole time. Is it ethical to keep this alchemy in a 25-day restorative guide?

Oh, it only works if I keep consistent appointments week after week. I may, as a member of the human species, not know where my tongue is supposed to be resting, but I know when I'm caught in a sales funnel.  And yet nothing else works... 

## Alfalfa and the omega 

My father told me when he lived in California in the 70s, the big health craze was alfalfa sprouts. Avocados, almonds, veganism, oats, meats, and then protein in general have all had their moment. Yoga, running, then weightlifting and now Pilates again. I just want to know what's best forever. 

I don't know what's on the horizon, but every time I have some sprouts on a sandwich I think: *very underrated*.
