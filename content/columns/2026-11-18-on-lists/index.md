There is a list on my desk whose contents are so ordinary that it might as well be a piece of furniture. Still, I have kept it.

## The little rituals

I like the notion that a day can be shaped without being controlled. A list is a remarkably low-tech instrument for attempting just that.

### In the margins

Some items may never be crossed off. That has come to seem less like a failure than a form of continuity.
