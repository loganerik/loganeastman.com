On the way home, I noticed a small thing that appeared twice. At first this struck me as an accident. Then it struck me as the beginning of an argument with myself.

## The trouble with paying attention

There are so many things in the world that have no connection to one another. That observation is both obvious and strangely difficult to accept.

### A minor theory of everything

Perhaps one of the pleasures of writing is being allowed to take the unnecessary seriously, for a paragraph or two.

{{< pullquote >}}The ordinary begins to look extraordinary after enough patient attention.{{< /pullquote >}}
