The best technology, I am increasingly convinced, does not demand more of our attention. It protects it. A simple timer, a recurring note, a set place for keys: none are likely to be immortalized as great inventions.

## A modest theory of tools

A useful machine should leave a little more room for the day than it occupies. Of course, most of the machines around us now seem designed to occupy the entire day.

### On the subject of distraction

A small thing, done repeatedly, can begin to resemble an architecture. That is what makes routines interesting to me: they are the buildings we inhabit without noticing.

{{< aside >}}A minor exception: I remain helpless before a particularly good mechanical clock.{{< /aside >}}

## What remains

I would like more objects in my life that end the moment they have done their job.
