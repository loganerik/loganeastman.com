## The Task

Elevate a leading child abuse prevention nonprofit in a space that struggles to transcend its cause.

## The Solution

Position the brand as an uplifting thought leader driving change through "Positive Prevention."

## The Result

Thought leadership messaging complimented rebrand from "Children's Bureau" to "All For Kids." Staff was trained to become advocates of the conversation.
