## The Task

Position an innovative intergenerational healing company to a difficult high-net-worth audience who often focus on problematic individuals rather than the family ecosystem.

## The Solution

Appeal to target audience through a manifesto that asks, "What price would you put on having a healthy and happy family?"

## The Result

Resonant tagline, manifesto, key messaging, and other tactical copy drove further project scope of a website refresh.
