## The Task

Make controlled environmental agriculture (CEA) more attractive to investors, community decision-makers, and customers.

## The Solution

Focus on what people on the ground care about — food and jobs — by answering a simple question, "What if we could create the future we want from the food we need."

## The Result

Investor pitch deck became the groundwork for consumer-facing messaging. Vertical Harvest expanded from Jackson, WY to Westbrook, ME.
