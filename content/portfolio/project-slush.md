## The Task

Write a film script to inspire affiliate membership for a cannabis start-up.

## The Solution

Showcase the brand's nicer, gentler and more fun high while avoiding anxiety and negative effects of other types, through an ambassador strategy that offered simplicity compared to competitors.

## The Result

The brand successfully launched as a lifestyle brand.
